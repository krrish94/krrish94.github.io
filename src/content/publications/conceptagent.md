abstract: Robotic planning and execution in open-world environments is challenging because of vast state spaces and highly variable task embodiment. ConceptAgent is a natural-language-driven robotic platform that combines predicate grounding, embodied LLM-guided Monte Carlo Tree Search with self-reflection, dynamic language-aligned 3D scene graphs, and large multimodal models to improve reliable task execution in unstructured environments.
